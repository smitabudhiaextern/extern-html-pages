The Investment Thesis

What an investment thesis actually is

Every VC fund gets pitched thousands of startups. They can't look at all of them. So they write down, in advance, what they will and won't consider. That written filter is the investment thesis.

Think of it like a job posting, but in reverse: the fund describing its dream startup.

The Investment Thesis

The two tiers

Here's the secret that makes any thesis easy to read. Every line in it belongs to one of two tiers:

Tier 1: Must pass

Hard rules. If a startup breaks even one, it's out. No exceptions.

Tier 2: Nice to have

Bonus signals that make HP more excited about a startup that already passed Tier 1. They break ties. They never rescue a startup that failed Tier 1.
