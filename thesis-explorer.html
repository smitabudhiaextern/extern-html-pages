The brains of AI — built, shrunk, and reinvented.
A model is a chef who spent years in culinary school: all the knowledge, none of the kitchen. This category funds better chefs — smaller ones that fit on your laptop, specialized ones fine-tuned for one cuisine, and entirely new kinds of cooking.
Edge AI modelsFine-tuningNew breakthroughsPost-transformer (SSMs, world models)

Why it matters: today's giant models live in data centers. A soldier off-grid, a rural clinic, a self-driving car — they all need AI that runs on the device. Whoever cracks that owns an enormous market.

✅ Pick Models if you're curious how AI brains get built — and rebuilt.
The engine room — where AI actually runs.
Training a model is building the restaurant kitchen — a one-time cost. Inference is every meal cooked in it, forever. That's why inference is where most of the money in AI goes.
Edge AI (runtime, optimization)Hybrid AI (routing, edge-cloud)

Every ChatGPT answer you've ever read was inference happening in real time. Making it faster, cheaper, and smarter about where it runs (your device? the cloud? both?) is one of the biggest business problems in AI.

✅ Pick Inference if you're curious how AI runs — and what it costs.
Where AI meets the real world.
Models are the chef, inference is the kitchen — applications are the restaurant. It's the only layer customers ever see, and it's where the money changes hands.
AI-first appsAI agentsVertical AI (healthcare, retail, defense)Physical AI & robotics

Today's AI apps answer questions. The next wave acts — booking flights, running workflows, negotiating bills. The wave after that moves through the physical world: robots, drones, autonomous vehicles. We're in chapter one.

✅ Pick Applications if you're curious what AI does for people.
