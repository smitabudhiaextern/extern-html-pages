Designing dim_product
What goes in dim_product?

dim_product is your menu — one row per product.

Think of a pizza shop's menu: it lists each pizza once, with only what the pizza is — its name, its category. What changes every night — how many sold, what each cost after a coupon — never goes on the menu; that's tracked per order. dim_product works the same way. For every detail, ask one question: does it describe the product, or does it change with every sale?
