Did the ads actually work?

In the first half of the year, Breaking Games spent $37,000 on Facebook ads. Those ads are linked to $61,000 in sales.

Your gut call — did those ads make money?

The analyst's answer

$61K against $37K looks like profit. But that headline number quietly ignores three things: what the games actually cost to make and ship, the refunds and abandoned carts hiding in the data, and the biggest trap of all — whether those ads truly drove the sales, or just took the credit for buyers who'd have come anyway.

Two numbers can't settle it. Pinning down the real answer — which ads paid off, which customers are even real — is your job over the next six weeks.
