− Change in NWC

As revenue grows, more cash gets tied up in accounts receivable (customers owing money) and inventory. AP (what IES owes suppliers) partially offsets it.

Net effect: cash leaves the bank even though the business is profitable.

How NWC was modeled · DSO / DIO / DPO method
DSO 72 days
DIO 0 days
DPO 62 days

IES collects in 72 days, holds essentially zero inventory (IT services), and pays suppliers in 62 days. The 10-day gap = working capital drag.
