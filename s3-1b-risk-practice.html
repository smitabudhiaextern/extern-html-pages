Get a Feel for Risk Scores

Rating a threat comes down to two everyday questions: how likely is it, and how bad would it be. You already think this way all the time. Let's warm up on a couple of things that have nothing to do with security, then you'll rate real threats with the same move.

This part is optional. If likelihood times impact already feels obvious, skip ahead.

Warm-up 1 of 2 · Everyday Risk

Should You Grab the Umbrella?

Dark clouds are rolling in, and you are about to head out with your laptop in an open tote bag.
How likely is rain?
How bad if the laptop gets wet?

Pick both, and your score lights up.
Warm-up 2 of 2 · Everyday Risk

Charge the Phone Now, or Later?

Your phone is at 4 percent, and you are waiting on a call about a job you really want.
How likely is the phone to die before the call?
How bad if you miss that call?

Pick both, and your score lights up.

You Already Know How to Rank Risk

See the pattern? Likely plus costly jumps to the top of the list. Unlikely plus harmless can wait. Everything else sits in between. There is no single right answer, only a rating you can explain.
