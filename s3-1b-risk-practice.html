Get a Feel for Risk Scores

Rating a threat comes down to two everyday questions: how likely is it, and how bad would it be. You already think this way all the time. Let's warm up on a couple of everyday examples. After you pick, you'll see a common call to check yourself against.

This part is optional. If likelihood times impact already feels obvious, skip ahead.

Warm-up 1 of 2 · Everyday Risk

Should You Grab the Umbrella?

Grey clouds are gathering, and you are about to head out with your laptop in an open tote bag.
How likely is rain?
How bad if the laptop gets soaked?

Pick both, and your score lights up.
Warm-up 2 of 2 · Everyday Risk

Pour the Milk, or Toss It?

The milk is one day past its best-by date. You are about to pour it on your cereal.
How likely is the milk actually spoiled?
How bad if it is off and you drink it?

Pick both, and your score lights up.

You Already Know How to Rank Risk

See the pattern? Likely plus costly jumps to the top of the list. Unlikely plus harmless can wait. The common call is there to check yourself against, but it is not a fixed right answer. What matters is a rating you can explain.
