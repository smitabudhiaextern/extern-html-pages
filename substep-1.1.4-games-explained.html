Meet the Games

Three games, three completely different players. Here’s the gist of each before the numbers show up.

POOP: The Game

Party / Light $15–$30
What it is A fast, silly card game where you race to get rid of your cards — themed, start to finish, around needing the bathroom.
Who buys it Casual and gift buyers. It sells on its name and box art, mostly on impulse.

Sparkle*Kitty

Party / Light $15–$30
What it is A cheeky party game where cursed princesses break out of a tower by playing spell cards.
Who buys it Party players and series fans — it has a loyal following and lots of expansions.

Dwellings of Eldervale

Strategy $40–$200+
What it is A big fantasy strategy game — place your creatures, claim regions of a magical world, and battle for control. It comes with miniatures.
Who buys it Dedicated hobbyists who research before buying. It’s the revenue heavyweight of the catalog.
