The screen on two real EIC investments
ET4M EIC Heritage portfolio
What they do
Pumps a light petroleum liquid down old shale wells, lets it soak to loosen the oil that's stuck, then pumps the oil back up, reusing the same liquid on the next well.
Application The gateAn operator gets roughly twice the oil from a well they already drilled, no new drilling. Produce more, spend less.
StageEarly-stage, right in EIC's range.
LocationUS-based, working wells in Oklahoma and Texas.
Fit A textbook upstream win: more oil from wells that already exist.
Moleaer EIC Heritage portfolio
What they do
Makes a machine that fills liquid with trillions of tiny gas bubbles. In an oilfield, those bubbles help push more oil loose underground and help clean the water that comes up with it, with no added chemicals.
Application The gateTwo operator wins at once: more oil from existing wells, and cheaper handling of the dirty water that comes up with it.
StageMore established than EIC's usual early range, but the application was strong enough to back anyway.
LocationUS-based, in California.
Fit Strong enough on application that the later stage didn't stop it.

Same test both times: would an operator actually use it? For both, yes. Moleaer even shows application can outweigh a later stage.
