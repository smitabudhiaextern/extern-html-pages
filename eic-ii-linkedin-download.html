Save LinkedIn profiles as PDF Step 1 of 5
1 Find the leaders

Search the company name on LinkedIn, open its company page, and click the People tab. Look for the senior leaders: CEO, CTO, CFO. If the People tab is thin, Google site:linkedin.com/in "[Company name]" CEO.

On the company page, click the People tab.
Example only — the company shown (Vitro3D) is an illustration; you'll do this for your own company.
Spot the senior leaders (the CEO is boxed here).
Example only — your results will show your company's team.
2 Open each leader's profile
Click into each profile

Open the profile of each senior leader you want to save. Start with the CEO and CTO, then add the CFO or other C-level names if you need them.

3 Save the profile as a PDF
Reliable way (works everywhere) Press Ctrl + P (Windows) or Cmd + P (Mac), then choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.
Cleaner alternative On the profile, click "More" and choose "Save to PDF" if it appears.
4 Name each file clearly
Use one consistent format

Name every file [Company]_LinkedIn_[Role].pdf so you can tell them apart at a glance.

Phaidra_LinkedIn_CEO.pdf Phaidra_LinkedIn_CTO.pdf
5 Keep them together
Save all the PDFs in one folder

Drop every profile PDF into a single folder. You'll attach them to Claude in the next step.

Tip You usually only need the top 3–4 people (CEO, CTO, CFO). No need to grab the whole company.
