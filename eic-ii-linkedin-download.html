Save the Leadership Team's LinkedIn Profiles

Find a company's senior leaders on LinkedIn and save each profile as a PDF, so you can hand the team's real backgrounds to an AI later.

  1. 1

    Find the leaders

    On LinkedIn, type the company name in the search bar and open the company page. Click the "People" tab to see who works there, and look for the senior leaders: CEO, CTO, CFO, and any other C-level names.

    If the People tab is thin Search Google for site:linkedin.com/in "[Company name]" CEO (swap in CTO, CFO, and so on). That surfaces the individual profile pages directly.
  2. 2

    Open each leader's profile

    Click into the profile of each senior leader you want. Start with the CEO and CTO.

  3. 3

    Save the profile as a PDF

    Reliable way (works everywhere) Press Ctrl + P (Windows) or Cmd + P (Mac) to open Print, then choose "Save as PDF" as the destination and save.
    Cleaner alternative On the profile, click the "More" button under the person's name and choose "Save to PDF" if it appears. This gives a tidy resume-style file.
  4. 4

    Name each file clearly

    Use a consistent format so you can tell them apart: [Company]_LinkedIn_[Role].pdf. For example, Phaidra_LinkedIn_CEO.pdf and Phaidra_LinkedIn_CTO.pdf.

  5. 5

    Keep them together

    Save all the PDFs in one folder. You'll attach them to Claude in the next step so it can read the team's real backgrounds.

Tip: You usually only need the top 3–4 people (CEO, CTO, CFO). Don't download the whole company. The senior leaders are what matter for judging the team.
