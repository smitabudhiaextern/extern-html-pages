Where Mozilla's money comes from

Everything Mozilla took in during the year that ended December 31, 2024.

$680.4 millioncame in altogether. Here is how it arrived.

Point at a bar, tap it, or press Tab to reach it, and the box below says what that money is.

Royalties

Money paid to Mozilla as a share of what somebody else earns. This is the search money. A search engine pays Mozilla to be the one already set in Firefox, and the payment is a slice of what that engine earns from Firefox users' searches.

Interest and dividend income

Mozilla holds a large amount of savings, about $1.4 billion at the end of 2024. This is what those savings earned by sitting there. It is not money from customers.

Subscription and advertising revenue

People paying for extra products, plus advertising. This line is falling, not rising: $75.7 million in 2022, $64.8 million in 2023, $66.4 million in 2024. This is the money Mozilla has been trying to grow so it depends less on search, and it is smaller than it was two years earlier.

Contributions

Money people give to Mozilla because they want to help. They are not buying anything.

Everything else

Small remaining lines: other revenue, currency changes, and other income. We added them together into one bar so the five bars add up to the total.
