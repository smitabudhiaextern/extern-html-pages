The Six Things
You Change

The template is already built. These are the only parts of it you touch. Everything else in the file stays exactly as it is.

The whole job

Six regions, and nothing else

1 2 3 6 4 5
1 Header Your name and the date you built the numbers 2 edits
2 KPI cards Five numbers, plus one label for your own KPI 6 edits
3 Product leaderboard Your rows from dashboard_leaderboard 5 or 10 rows
4 Key Findings The sentences you locked in section 6.3 3 edits
5 Recommendations Three actions Mike can take on Monday 3 edits
6 Chart data Two arrays at the bottom of the file next module

Every one of them is marked in the file with a STUDENT comment.

Region 1 of 6

Header: your name and the date

On the page

In the file

Prepared by: <strong><!-- STUDENT: Your Name --></strong>
Date: <strong><!-- STUDENT: Today's Date --></strong>
Use the date you ran your queries, not the date of the meeting. Step 1 asked you to record when the numbers were built. A dashboard with no date is one nobody can check.

Region 2 of 6

KPI cards: the comment sits inside the line

On the page

In the file, for the first card

<div class="kpi-value">$<!-- STUDENT: total_revenue -->61,085</div>
The comment names which number belongs there, using the column name from dashboard_metrics. Replace the value that comes after it, not below it, and leave the comment in place.

Region 2, continued

Your own KPI needs a new label too

On the page

One of your five is the KPI you chose yourself and marked ★ in section 6.2. On that card the label is wrong as well as the number, and the label is the part students forget.

Before

<div class="kpi-value">
  <!-- STUDENT: top_product_share -->22%
</div>
<div class="kpi-label">Top Product Share of Revenue</div>

After

<div class="kpi-value">
  <!-- STUDENT: discount_usage_pct -->6.8%
</div>
<div class="kpi-label">Carts Using a Discount Code</div>

A number sitting under somebody else's label is the fastest way to lose the room.

Region 3 of 6

Leaderboard: one row per product

On the page

In the file

<tr><td>1</td><td>Dwellings of Eldervale 2nd Edition: Standard</td><td>$13,323.56</td><td>129</td><td>$103.28</td></tr>
Five cells in order: rank, product, revenue, units, AOV. Rows 1 to 3 are examples, rows 4 to 10 say [Replace me]. If you built yours with LIMIT 5, delete rows 6 to 10 instead of leaving them.

Region 4 of 6

Key Findings: one sentence per paragraph

On the page

In the file

<p class="mb-2">
  <!-- STUDENT: Write your first finding here. -->
  [Your first key finding]
</p>
Replace the bracketed line with a finding from section 6.3. One sentence per paragraph, three paragraphs, three findings.

Region 5 of 6

Recommendations: blank until you write them

On the page

In the file

<li><!-- STUDENT: Reorder priority --></li>
<li><!-- STUDENT: Ad spend reallocation --></li>
<li><!-- STUDENT: One fix for cart abandonment --></li>
That screenshot is the template exactly as it downloads. A comment renders as nothing, so those three bullets really are empty. Write inside each <li>, after the comment.

Region 6 of 6

Chart data comes next

Both charts are already drawn and already styled with placeholder numbers. To make them show yours, you change two data arrays in the script block at the bottom of the file. That is the whole of the next module, so leave them alone for now.

The other five are done when: no [Replace me] is left, no bullet is empty, the header carries your name and date, and every number matches what SELECT * FROM dashboard_metrics; gave you.

Then open the file in your browser and read it the way Shari will, from the top, for ninety seconds.
