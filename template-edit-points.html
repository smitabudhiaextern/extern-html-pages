The Six Things
You Change

The template is already built. These are the only parts of it you touch. Everything else in the file stays exactly as it is.

The whole job

Six regions, and nothing else

1 Header Your name and the date you built the numbers 2 edits
2 KPI cards Five numbers, plus one label for your own KPI 6 edits
3 Product leaderboard Your rows from dashboard_leaderboard 5 or 10 rows
4 Key Findings The sentences you locked in section 6.3 3 edits
5 Recommendations Three actions Mike can take on Monday 3 edits
6 Chart data Two arrays at the bottom of the file next module

Every one of them is marked in the file with a STUDENT comment.

Region 1 of 6

Header: your name and the date

In the file

Prepared by: <strong><!-- STUDENT: Your Name --></strong>
Date: <strong><!-- STUDENT: Today's Date --></strong>

Write your name and the date between the <strong> tags, after the comment.

Use the date you ran your queries, not the date of the meeting. Step 1 asked you to record when the numbers were built. A dashboard with no date is one nobody can check.

Region 2 of 6

KPI cards: the comment sits inside the line

In the file

<div class="kpi-value">$<!-- STUDENT: total_revenue -->61,085</div>
<div class="kpi-label">Total Revenue (Jan–Jul)</div>

The comment names which of your numbers belongs there. Here it is total_revenue, a column from dashboard_metrics.

Replace the value that comes after the comment, not below it. Leave the comment where it is, so you can find your way back.

Region 2, continued

Your own KPI needs a new label too

One of your five is the KPI you chose yourself and marked ★ in section 6.2. For that card, the label is wrong as well as the number.

Before

<div class="kpi-value">
  <!-- STUDENT: top_product_share -->22%
</div>
<div class="kpi-label">
  Top Product Share of Revenue
</div>

After

<div class="kpi-value">
  <!-- STUDENT: discount_usage_pct -->6.8%
</div>
<div class="kpi-label">
  Carts Using a Discount Code
</div>

A number sitting under somebody else's label is the fastest way to lose the room.

Region 3 of 6

Leaderboard: one row per product

In the file

<tr><td>1</td><td>Dwellings of Eldervale 2nd Edition: Standard</td><td>$13,323.56</td><td>129</td><td>$103.28</td></tr>
<tr><td>4</td><td>[Replace me]</td><td>$0</td><td>0</td><td>$0</td></tr>

Five cells, always in this order: rank, product, revenue, units, AOV. That is the same order your dashboard_leaderboard query returns them.

Rows 1 to 3 are filled in as an example. Rows 4 to 10 say [Replace me]. If you built yours with LIMIT 5, delete rows 6 to 10 rather than leaving them on the page.

Region 4 of 6

Key Findings: one sentence per paragraph

In the file

<p class="mb-2">
  <!-- STUDENT: Write your first finding here. -->
  [Your first key finding]
</p>

Replace the bracketed line with a finding you already wrote in section 6.3. There are three of these paragraphs, so you need three findings.

One sentence per paragraph. Do not stack two findings into one <p> to fit them all in.

Region 5 of 6

Recommendations: blank until you write them

In the file

<li><!-- STUDENT: Reorder priority --></li>
<li><!-- STUDENT: Ad spend reallocation --></li>
<li><!-- STUDENT: One fix for cart abandonment --></li>

These three are the only region with nothing in them at all. Write inside each <li>, after the comment.

A comment renders as nothing, so right now your page shows three empty bullets. It is the easiest thing on the whole dashboard to forget.

Region 6 of 6

Chart data comes next

Both charts are already drawn and already styled. To make them show your numbers you change two data arrays in the script block at the bottom of the file. That is the whole of the next module, so leave them for now.

You are done with the other five when: no [Replace me] is left anywhere, no bullet is empty, the header carries your name and date, and every number matches what SELECT * FROM dashboard_metrics; gave you.

Open the file in your browser one more time and read it the way Shari will, from the top, for ninety seconds.
