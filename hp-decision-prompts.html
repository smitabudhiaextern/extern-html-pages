Prompt 1 of 3

Set up the investor

HP's thesis is already in here. Just copy and paste. 
You're a Venture Capital investor at HP Tech Ventures (https://hptechventures.com/).
Judge startups against this thesis.

TIER 1, the hard rules. A startup must pass all three or it's out.
1. Where: US-based, or global but does business with the US.
2. Size: raised between $1M and $40M, valued under about $200M.
3. What: builds AI technology in Models, Inference, or Applications.

TIER 2, tie-breakers only. These never rescue a startup that failed Tier 1.
Tech that's hard to copy. A market over $1B or fast-growing customers.
Founders from strong schools, known accelerators, or big-name companies.
Well-known investors, patents, or serious media coverage.

I'll share 3 startups with you. Evaluate them, pick 1 to research deeply, and
reject the other 2. Tell me when you're ready.
This is the same thesis you learned in the thesis read. Wait for it to reply before you send prompt 2.
Prompt 2 of 3

Hand over your three

Copy your three rows straight out of your shortlist sheet, whatever columns you filled in.

Paste this into your AI 
Here's what I have on my 3 companies:

[PASTE YOUR 3 ROWS FROM YOUR SHORTLIST SHEET HERE]

Visit each company's website. Then go company by company, and use what you find
on the website plus the information above to give me your investor point of view
on that company.

Start with the first company. Finish it before you move to the next one.
Why one at a time? Judging three at once makes an AI mix their numbers up, attaching one company's funding to another. Slowing it down keeps the facts where they belong.
Skim what it says about each company as it goes.
Prompt 3 of 3

Make it show its work

If it just names a winner, you've got nothing to push back on. Asking for a rubric and scores gives you something you can actually argue with.

Paste this into your AI 
If you had to pick one company to research further out of these three, which one
would it be?

Answer using this exact process:
1. Define an evaluation rubric.
2. Score each company on every dimension of that rubric.
3. Say which company is the best fit, and why you rejected the other two.

Check each company against the Tier 1 rules explicitly. For every number you use,
tell me where it came from.
Keep this answer open. You'll check it in the next task, and you're allowed to overrule it.
That's all three prompts.

Before you move on

What you've got back is a recommendation.

The AI hasn't opened these websites the way you have, and it doesn't have to live with the answer. You do. You'll spend Projects 2, 3 and 4 on whichever company you pick, so you're allowed to disagree with it.

Two things happen next. You check the pick against HP's Tier 1 rules, which takes about a minute. Then you commit to a company and Project 1 is done.
If you'd rather take your second choice, that's a legitimate call. Just say why in the task.
