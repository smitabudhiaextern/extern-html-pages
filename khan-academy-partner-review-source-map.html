[Externship] Khan Academy Strategic Partnerships & Earned Revenue Externship
Khan Academy is free for more than 100 million learners a year. Most of that is funded by donations. The rest it earns, and a small strategic partnerships team is responsible for finding the deals that bring it in. In this externship, working as an analyst on that team, you'll spend 7 weeks taking apart the partnerships Khan Academy already runs, studying how organizations like Wikimedia, Duolingo and Mozilla earn from free products, and finding partners worth pitching.
