Honest read · the calls behind the calls

What stands out across all 13.

Look at the 13 drivers side by side and a shape emerges. Where the analyst leaned aggressive, where they leaned conservative, and where they punted on complexity.

Aggressive
Gross Margin +100 bps/year for 4 years. The most-leaned-on assumption in the model. Drives most of the operating income lift — needs a real thesis (cost takeouts, recurring-mix shift, pricing power) to defend.
Aggressive
ES Adj EBITDA Margin growing every year (+25 bps/yr). Combined with the gross-margin call, the analyst is stacking two layers of margin expansion. If gross margin underperforms, the segment margin call typically goes with it.
Conservative
Working capital held flat at FY24 actuals. Receivables and payables ratios don't move for 5 years. No thesis on collections improvement, no thesis on stretching suppliers — the analyst left a lever untouched.
Telling
Interest rate hardcoded at 4.7% and carried forward. A single anchor across the full projection window. Refinancing dates, rate changes, or new debt issuance would all break this — but the analyst chose to keep it simple.
