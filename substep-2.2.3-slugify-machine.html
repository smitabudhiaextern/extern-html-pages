Building the Slug
What's a slug?

A slug is the clean, URL-safe version of a name.

A web address can't handle capitals, spaces, or symbols like * and :. Here's a real product title that breaks every one of those rules:

Messy product title
Sparkle*Kitty Nights: Deluxe
Capitals, the * and :, and spaces — a URL can't use any of it.
A slug fixes all three:
Lowercase everything — no capitals.
Strip the punctuation — out go the * and :.
Spaces become hyphens — one dash between words.

The query

It's just those four steps, stacked.

SQLite has no slugify function, so you nest the functions you just used. You won't write this from scratch — Claude is excellent at exactly this kind of query. Your job is just to read it inside-out and recognize each step.

UPDATE dim_product SET product_slug = LOWER( REPLACE(REPLACE(REPLACE(product_title, ' ', '-'), ':', ''), '*', '') );
A real title
Dwellings of Eldervale 2nd Edition: Standard
Its slug
dwellings-of-eldervale-2nd-edition-standard
This isn't perfect — a few odd titles will still need manual cleanup. A ~80–90% match rate is plenty for analytics, and you'll fix the stragglers by hand.
The rule
A slug = lowercase + no punctuation + spaces become hyphens.
LOWER REPLACE punctuation REPLACE spaces
Run it once across the whole column, then fix the handful of stragglers by hand. That's all a slug column takes.
