Cadence budget

Max 3 touches/week in weeks 1–2 of a stall, then 1/week. Never 2 within 48h. Max 3 messages per stall, then silence.

Channel

Email only (v1). Slack DMs removed by decision Jul 14 — reserved as an iteration-2 test if email wins. Every message is PM-signed with a reply invitation.

Menu email wildcard

Student opened but didn't act? Touch 2 may be swapped for the menu email above — 5 self-serve fixes, every click logs the barrier.

Suppression

No playful copy to students with personal-circumstance support tickets. No touches to PM-contacted students, the global family holdout, or the control group. R5 arm recorded per student.
