University Tech-Transfer IP

★ EIC especially wants this

Patented inventions from university labs, professors and postdocs, that no one has built a company around yet. They go on your map as pre-company entries.

Why it matters

This early IP is hard to find. It does not turn up in a normal search, and it is not on every investor's radar, so surfacing it is a real discovery, not a name everyone already has.

That is what makes it valuable to an investor. The well-known startups are already on everyone's list, so those deals tend to get crowded. University IP that has not become a company yet is different: whoever finds it gets the first look, can get in at the very start before the price climbs, and can even help shape the company from day one. And because it is already patented, it is hard for anyone else to copy. Find a few strong ones and you are handing EIC an edge no one else has.

What to record

Inventing institution Patent status How ready it is to commercialize

How to find it

  • 1
    Ask AI
    "Find recent university research on [your subsector] that has been patented but not yet turned into a company. For each, name the university or lab, the professor if known, what the invention does in one line, and the patent or paper as a source."
  • 2
    University tech-transfer pages

    Many universities post their patented inventions on a technology transfer or "available technologies" page.

  • 3
    Google Patents

    Search for inventions owned by a university.
