If you are stuck Six that fit the thesis

Six startups that fit HP's thesis

Any of these will get you through to the next step. Worth knowing the trade, though: other externs are reading this same page, so a few of you will land on the same company. If you have twenty more minutes in you, finding your own is the better deal.

Models
Fastino
fastino.ai
Small AI models, each trained to do one job well. They trained theirs on cheap gaming GPUs.
Inference
Luminal
luminal.com
Rewrites AI code so it runs faster on the graphics cards you already own.
OpenInfer
openinfer.io
Runs AI on your own machines instead of sending your data to the cloud.
ElastixAI
elastix.ai
Uses cheaper FPGA chips instead of GPUs to run large language models.
Applications
Simular
simular.ai
An AI that drives your computer, clicking and typing across desktop apps.
Layer Health
layerhealth.com
Reads a patient's full medical record and pulls out the facts a hospital needs.

Check the funding yourself. Startups raise money fast, so one that fits today may be too big next month. Confirming that is part of the job.
