This interactive uses four real Breaking Games games. Put traffic (pageviews) on the horizontal axis and sales (units) on the vertical axis, and four kinds of game separate out.

The four quadrants

High traffic and high sales is a Flagship — keep reordering. High traffic but low sales is one to Investigate — don't cut it, something's off. Low traffic but high sales is a Hidden winner — promote it. Low traffic and low sales — let it ride.
