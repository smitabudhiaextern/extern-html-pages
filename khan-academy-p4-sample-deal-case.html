Worked example · Project 4 · one deal, all seven fields

Microsoft, written before the deal

This is one finished deal case. Khan Academy has a paid product for teachers and the thing making it paid is the cost of running it. Microsoft owns the kind of computing power that cost is made of. The case below argues that Khan Academy should ask Microsoft for that computing power instead of for money, and it answers all seven questions the team's checklist asks of a deal.

Decision requested

Authorize a first conversation with Microsoft Education: ask for donated infrastructure, not cash, and offer content and math expertise back.

Written as the case that would have won the May 2024 deal, then checked against what the record now shows.

How to read this page, and how to reuse it

Read it for how it is put together, not for what it concludes. You are not being asked to agree with this recommendation. You are being asked to see the shape: seven questions, answered in order, with the evidence and the argument kept visibly apart.

It covers one deal. You have two. Run the same treatment twice, once per deal, so do not count the sections here and think the ask has changed.

Every claim on this page is one of two things. A sourced fact, with a live link and the period the figure covers. Or the analyst's own inference, which is an opinion built on the facts and is labelled as one. The two look different on purpose, and they differ by six things at once so that you never have to guess which you are reading.

The case argues for a deal that has not happened yet. Field 2 of 7

The partnership model I recommend

Name the shape, not just the partner. This one has two halves and they run at the same time.

Half one

An in-kind donation of computing power

In-kind means the partner gives a thing rather than money. Here the thing is donated access to Azure AI-optimized infrastructure, which is Microsoft's own published wording for it, and Azure is the name of Microsoft's cloud. Khan Academy asks for the computing power that runs Khanmigo instead of asking for a cheque.

Why ask for the thing and not the money. A cheque has to be spent, budgeted, and defended again next year. Computing power arrives as the exact cost it displaces, which is the cost that is keeping the teacher product paid.

Half two

Technical co-development on a smaller math model

Khan Academy contributes explanatory content and step-by-step math answers to Microsoft's Phi-3 work, which is Microsoft's family of small language models. No Khan Academy user data goes into training that model. Alongside it, both sides work to put more Khan Academy content into Copilot and Teams for Education, which are Microsoft products teachers already have open.

Why the second half matters. Without it this is a donation, and a donation is somebody's goodwill. With it, both sides are shipping something, which is a different kind of relationship to renew.

Sources for the model, all published on the same day: Microsoft Source, 21 May 2024 for the donated-access wording, for the Phi-3 content and step-by-step math answers, for the sentence that none of Khan Academy's user data will be used to train the model, and for the intent to integrate more Khan Academy content into Copilot and Teams for Education. Khan Academy blog, 21 May 2024 for the teacher tools going free with Microsoft's support. Period covered: the arrangement as announced on 21 May 2024.

A rough outline of the first conversation

Five beats, one line each. This is the only part of the case that a person outside Khan Academy would hear, so it drops the internal reasoning and it never opens by pleading.

  1. The ask, in one sentence. We are asking for infrastructure rather than cash, because the cost of running Khanmigo is the thing standing between the teacher tools and free.

  2. The shared goal. A free, world-class education to anyone, anywhere, and an invitation to Microsoft to help make the teacher half of it free.

    The mission wording is quoted from Khan Academy's About page, checked 7 September 2026. It is to anyone, anywhere.

  3. What each side gives and gets. Your infrastructure frees the teacher tools; our explanatory content and step-by-step math answers go to your smaller math model, with none of our user data in the training.

    Source: Microsoft Source, 21 May 2024. Period covered: the price teachers paid up to May 2024. What stays inside, and what a partner may ever see

Field 6 of 7

Risks and alternatives

A risk is something that could go wrong if we do this deal. An alternative is somebody else doing it instead, which is a different question and gets its own block below. A mitigation is the answer to a risk, and it has to name who does what before the meeting. “Move first and shape the terms” is not a mitigation, it is a reason to hurry.

Risk 1 of 3 · student data privacy

This deal places a commercial cloud provider's infrastructure under an artificial intelligence tutor that works with children.

Khan Academy has signed the Student Privacy Pledge since 2015, which is a public promise about how a company handles data about students.

Sources: Khan Academy joined the pledge in January 2015 (Washington Post, 20 January 2015). Period covered, and it matters: the Future of Privacy Forum retired that pledge on 25 April 2025 and maintained the signatory list only through 31 July 2025 (Future of Privacy Forum), and commitments remain binding for data collected while a signatory. So nobody is a live signatory today, and this risk cannot be answered by pointing at the pledge as though it were still running.

How I would answer it

Make the exclusion a deal term: no user data in any model training. The announced deal states exactly this (Microsoft Source, 21 May 2024). The Student Privacy Pledge is ours to keep, and no counterparty's paperwork keeps it for us, so the contract terms have to let us keep it. Who does what before the meeting: the analyst drafts those data terms, which is step 2 of the next steps at the foot of this page.

Note the scope of the source. The exclusion in the announcement covers training the model. It is not a general promise about storage, processing, telemetry or who can look at the data, so the drafted terms have to cover the rest of it.

Risk 2 of 3 · switching costs and concentration

Donated infrastructure buys our switching costs. One cloud, one model supplier, one donor.

Switching costs are what it would cost to leave. Once Khanmigo is rebuilt on somebody else's infrastructure, free has quietly bought the cost of moving, and that cost is real even though nobody ever invoices it.

Outside precedent, with its number and its period: Mozilla's own audited financial statements report that about 86 percent of its revenues from customers with contracts came from a single customer, and that the same customer accounted for 72 percent of outstanding receivables at year end, for the financial year ended 31 December 2024 (Mozilla, FY2024 audited financial statements). Mozilla is a different organization in a different market, and it is here as an example of how far concentration can go, not as a forecast of anything.

How I would answer it

Price the replacement before accepting, and keep Khanmigo able to run on more than one model. The number we want is what it would cost to run the same workload elsewhere, worked out on today's prices. Who does what before the meeting: the analyst prices the replacement cost, and engineering states in writing which parts of Khanmigo are tied to one supplier and which are not.

Risk 3 of 3 · donor renewal

A donation has no contractual floor. It can be reduced, or simply not renewed, and nothing has been breached.

This is the risk the shape brings with it. An in-kind donation is not a purchase order, so the thing keeping it in place next year is somebody at the partner still wanting it there.

Outside precedent, with its number and its period: Code.org, a nonprofit funded largely by corporate contributions, reported revenue of $25,176,713 against expenses that produced a deficit of $6,675,480 for the twelve months ended 31 August 2025 (Forms 990, employer identification number 46-0858543, via ProPublica). No cause is asserted for that deficit here, because none is stated in the filing. Note also that Code.org changed its fiscal year end, so its periods are not directly comparable to each other in dollars.

How I would answer it

Ask for a stated term and a renegotiation date, in writing, in the first conversation. A donation with an end date on it can be planned around. A donation with no end date cannot. Who does what before the meeting: the partnerships lead puts the term and the renegotiation date on the agenda, so that it is asked for rather than hoped for.

A risk I cannot answer

Brand association.

Khan Academy sits in front of districts as a neutral party, and some of those districts are cautious about large technology companies in classrooms. Putting one of them under the tutor changes how that neutrality reads to some of them. I have no answer to attach to this one and no source that sizes it, so it is stated with nothing underneath it rather than dressed up with a mitigation that would not survive a question.

Who takes the seat if Khan Academy does not move

On our side of the table. If we do not ask, the alternatives are to buy the computing power out of our own budget, or to leave the teacher product at $4 per month. Both are available and neither is free: the first spends money we would rather spend on content, and the second keeps the paid teacher tier that Field 1 says is the gap in the mission sentence.

On Microsoft's side of the table. The seat we would be asking for could be filled by another education organization with math content to contribute. I could not name one from a source I reached. No public document found for this case names another education nonprofit in this arrangement with Microsoft, so I will not invent a name to make the block look complete. If somebody in the meeting knows one, that is a good question to be asked.

Why this block is not a risk row. Somebody else doing the deal instead is an option set, not something that goes wrong if we do it. Keeping the two apart is the difference between a risk section a reader trusts and one they stop reading.

The answer to the field, in one line

An established shape, not a first of its kind: three named arrangements, all sourced and dated, all unpriced. And the honest gap: no outside precedent carrying a public dollar figure for a donation of this shape and scale was found in the sources reached for this case. So this case is not priced in dollars, and Field 4 counts teachers instead. “We found no precedent that carries a number” is a complete answer to write down. “This is a first of its kind” is not, because it is a claim about every deal that has ever been done and no search can support it.
