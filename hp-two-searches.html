Two kinds of search

Step 1 of 3
Search 1

The wide net

You

AI, find me startups working in edge AI.

What comes back
Well-known startup Well-known startup Well-known startup

Fast, and a great start. But your whole cohort is asking something similar, so you tend to get the same famous companies.

Why this happens: AI learned from what people have already written about. The more famous a company is, the more the AI has read about it, so those are the names it reaches for first.

That is not a bug. It is a great way to get moving fast. It just means your first list looks a lot like everyone else's.

Search 2

The specific dig

Search 1 Find me startups working in edge AI.
Search 2 Search Product Hunt for edge AI launches from this year.

One word changed everything. You named the place you wanted AI to look.

Those places list brand new companies the moment they show up, long before anyone writes articles about them. So AI finds names it would not have reached for on its own.

What comes back
Something others missed Something others missed Something others missed

Same AI. You just pointed it at one specific place. This is where you find startups nobody else in your cohort has.

It works the same for a startup directory, Reddit, or a plain Google search. One place at a time.

The answer

You can visit these sites yourself.
You do not have to.

If you want to browse

Go for it. Clicking around a startup directory is genuinely interesting, and you might spot something AI skipped.

If you would rather not

Send AI in instead. Give it one place and one thing to look for, and it does the walking.

In practice: run search 1 first. If you already have three good startups, you are done. If you want more, or you want ones nobody else has, run search 2 on one of the places from your Search Stack.

Either way, one rule does not change. Open the real website of anything you find before it goes on your list.
