Know Your Competition Intro

Direct vs. indirect competitors

A competitor is anyone who could take your customer. They come in two kinds, direct and indirect. Let's sort a few for McDonald's.

Direct
Sells basically the same thing to the same customer.
Indirect
Meets the same need a different way.

The takeaway for a startup

Direct competitors

Chase your customer with a similar product or method.

Indirect competitors

Solve the same problem a different way, including the old way of doing things and doing nothing at all.

In the next step you'll sort real competitors on two questions: do they chase the same customer, and do they use the same core technology?
