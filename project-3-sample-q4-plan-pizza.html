Q4 Ad Allocation Plan — Tony's Pizzeria

Sample deliverable · what a finished plan looks like

Prepared for: Tony (owner)  ·  Decision: where the holiday ad budget goes  ·  Ad budget in question: ~$1,500

✅ Keep running

Ads that worked. Keep them on, and scale the best ones up.

Weekend Flyer Drop

Decision
Keep, and spend a bit more on it.
Backed by
$2.10 cost per customer — the cheapest of all Tony's ads.
Expected
More weekend orders for the same money.
Caveat
We only count a flyer when the customer mentions it. Some forget, so the real cost is probably even lower — not higher.

Instagram Boosted Post — "Meat Lovers"

Decision
Keep, and reuse this photo in other posts.
Backed by
3.1% of people who saw it tapped through — the highest of any ad.
Expected
The creative clearly grabs attention; reusing it should lift other posts too.
Caveat
A tap is not an order. High interest is a good sign, but it doesn't prove sales on its own.

✂️ Cut or pause

Ads that spent real money without much to show. Cutting them frees up budget.

Local Radio Spot

Decision
Cut.
Backed by
$410 spent, about 7 customers traced to it = ~$58 each. Way above the $2–5 the other ads cost.
Expected
Frees $410 with very little lost.
Caveat
Radio builds general awareness we can't fully measure. But at $58 a customer, the cost is too high to justify on orders alone.

Bus Stop Poster

Decision
Cut.
Backed by
$250 spent, 0 customers we could trace back to it in a full month.
Expected
Frees $250.
Caveat
A poster is hard to track by nature. Still, no signal at all after a month is a bad sign.

💡 New investment

Where the freed-up money should go. This one is judgment — no query hands it to you.

November "Family Holiday Deal" — flyer + Instagram

Decision
Start a new campaign with the freed budget.
Backed by
Flyers are Tony's cheapest channel, and November is his busiest month.
Expected
Puts new money behind the channel that already works, timed to the holiday rush.
Caveat
Brand-new campaign, no history yet. Start small and watch the cost-per-customer for the first two weeks before spending more.

💰 The money math

Cut Radio $410 + Bus Stop $250 = ~$660 freed

That freed $660 funds the November Family Holiday Deal — Tony's cheapest channel, aimed at his busiest month. No new money needed; it's paid for by cutting what wasn't working.
