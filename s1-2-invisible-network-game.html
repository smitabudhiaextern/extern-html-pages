The Invisible Network

Three everyday devices are hiding in plain sight. I'll describe each one in clues, you guess it, then we'll take it apart to see the four pieces inside. Finally, you'll look at it the way an attacker would.

You can now see the invisible network.

Every device you took apart runs on the same four parts: something that senses, a broker (the network) that carries the data out, a subscriber that reads it, and, in the dangerous ones, an actuator that acts on the real world. Broker is the word the pros use, and the one you'll use from here on. And you felt the stakes climb: your habits, then your voice, then your front door. The lesson the attacker already knows: a device's danger isn't what it knows, it's what it can do.
