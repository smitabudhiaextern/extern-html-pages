The answer

Dwellings of Eldervale

The strategy heavyweight. A $120+ box doesn't need to sell many copies to out-earn a stack of $15 party games — that's revenue concentration, the thing you'll measure for real in Week 3. POOP sells the most copies, but Dwellings earns the most dollars — that's the difference between units and revenue, and it's exactly the kind of thing you'll separate out in the data. That's your first analyst call — in Week 3 you'll run the real numbers and find out if you nailed it.
