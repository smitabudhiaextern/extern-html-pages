Project 3 Handover Video: Three Styles

The same approved script built in three styles, all in the Pfizer × Extern Core colors. The voice is a temporary computer voice. The ElevenLabs voice replaces it once a style is picked.

01

Hand-Drawn Ink, in the Style of "How the Economic Machine Works"

Wobbly ink lines that draw themselves, a simple stick-figure Ana and gentle pop-ins.

02

Clean Split Panels, in the Style of Stanford GSB's "What Is an Algorithm?"

Sharp split screens and wipes, with headlines revealed one word at a time.

03

One Continuous Line, in the Style of HBR's "The Explainer"

A single drawn line sketches each scene as the camera pans along to the next.
