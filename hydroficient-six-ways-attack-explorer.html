Maya Chen: "Here's what I want you to internalize. When I assess a new system, I mentally walk through each attack type. It becomes automatic. By the end of this externship, you'll think the same way."

Maya's mental checklist — ask this about every system you ever meet:
  • Could someone eavesdrop here? How?
  • What if they spoofed this sensor?
  • Where's the replay vulnerability?
  • Could anyone sit in the middle of this connection?
  • What happens when someone floods it?
  • What's the laziest way to just walk in?

Key insight: the "easiest" attacks — eavesdropping, DoS, default credentials — are the most common. Attackers prefer low-hanging fruit.
