How to find your competitors
A few quick ways to spot the companies chasing the same customer, then trim the list down to what matters.
1
Google. Type the technology (or the problem it solves) into Google. The companies at the top of the results, especially the ones paying to run ads on those keywords, are usually chasing the same customer. Jot down the names.
2
Perplexity or Claude. Ask directly: [company name] competitors and alternatives to [company name]. Good for a fast, sourced list you can cross-check against your Google names.
3
Crunchbase. Open the company's Crunchbase page and look at its "Similar Companies" list.
Find a handful, then keep only the 3–4 closest. You're not building a giant list, you want the few that truly compete.
