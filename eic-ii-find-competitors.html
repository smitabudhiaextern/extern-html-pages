Start with Perplexity or Claude
Start here. Ask an AI to surface the names, it's the fastest way in.
I'm evaluating [company] as a potential investment and need to map its competition. Here's what the company does: [explain the technology in 2-3 lines]. Using public sources, give me the real alternatives a customer has, in three groups: (1) direct competitors, chasing the same customer with a similar product or method; (2) indirect competitors, solving the same problem a different way; (3) the 'old way' of doing this, and doing nothing. For each, give the name, one line on what they do, and a source link. Focus on the 5 or 6 closest, not a long list.
Quick queries too: [company] competitors and alternatives to [company].
Next, see who's out there in the open. Search the problem the company solves:
[the problem or technology the company solves] alternatives to [company] best [product category]
The companies running ads and ranking at the top are usually chasing the same customer. Jot down the names.
Crunchbase is a company database that's handy for a startup's funding and stage. Use it to fill in the details on the competitors you already found.
Search a competitor's name, open its profile, and read the Overview and Funding sections to get its total funding, its stage (Seed, Series A, and so on), and its rough size. (Crunchbase's "Similar Companies" tab is a paid feature now, so use Crunchbase to get facts on names you already have, not to find new ones.)
For each of the 3 or 4 closest, jot down:
  • Name, and one line on what they do
  • Size / stage (and funding, if it's public)
  • Competes on the market? (same customer) — Yes / No
  • Competes on the core technology? (same method) — Yes / No
  • Their strength vs your company, and their weakness
  • Threat: High / Medium / Low
The two Yes/No lines do the real work: yes to both is a direct rival; yes on the market but no on the technology is indirect.
Find a handful, then keep only the 3 or 4 closest.
You're not building a giant list. You want the few that truly compete.
