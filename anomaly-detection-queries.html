Project 4 · Step 2 · Pattern Detection

Something Doesn't Add Up —
Spotting Anomalies

The senior analyst's superpower is noticing that something is off before you know exactly what. Here are the checks — copy each query, run it in DB Browser, and look at the shape of what comes back.

Pattern 1

The distribution that's too flat

Real shopping has rhythms — busy at some hours, quiet at others. If every hour has almost the same count, that flatness itself is the red flag: automation, not people.

Carts by hour of day 
SELECT
    substr(created_at, 12, 2) AS hour_of_day,
    COUNT(DISTINCT name) AS carts_started
FROM shopify_checkouts
GROUP BY hour_of_day
ORDER BY hour_of_day;
On Breaking Games' data this looks healthy — carts peak around lunchtime and evening, quiet overnight. That's normal. The opposite (every hour equal) is when you worry.

Pattern 2

Cart values that don't make sense

A cart with an extreme quantity of one item (say 6 or more) deserves a second look. Sometimes a real reseller; usually automation or test data.

Carts with 6+ of one item 
SELECT
    name, email, lineitem_name, lineitem_quantity, total
FROM shopify_checkouts
WHERE lineitem_quantity > 5
ORDER BY lineitem_quantity DESC;
Returns 27 rows on Breaking Games' data — real anomalies worth investigating.

From observation to diagnosis

Three questions

1

Is it a measurement glitch?

Could it come from how the data was exported or joined, not from real customers? Check this first.

2

Is it real but explainable?

A July 4th spike might be a sale; an odd country might be a wholesale partner. Real behavior with a real reason isn't a problem.

3

Is it real, unexplained, and big enough to matter?

If yes, you have a finding — document it, measure it, decide if it changes your recommendation.

The forensic template

A reusable 3-step dig

Once you spot a candidate, dig with three quick queries. Replace [pattern] with your condition, e.g. lineitem_quantity > 5.

Forensic 3-step template 
-- Step 1: How widespread is the pattern?
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM shopify_checkouts WHERE [pattern];

-- Step 2: Who exhibits it?
SELECT email, COUNT(*) FROM shopify_checkouts WHERE [pattern]
GROUP BY email ORDER BY 2 DESC;

-- Step 3: What's the impact if we exclude it?
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT name) FROM shopify_checkouts WHERE NOT [pattern];

Three queries, one pattern, a clear before-and-after.
