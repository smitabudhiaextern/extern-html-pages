01 / 10 · COVER
Category · Revenue Driver 1 of 8

Revenue

Projection formula
Projected Revenue = (1 + Revenue Growth %) × Prior Year Revenue

Maya's driver: 12% growth. So Summer 2 revenue = 1.12 × Summer 1 revenue.

02 / 10 · REVENUE
Category · Margins Driver 2 of 8

COGS

Projection formula
Projected COGS = (1 − Gross Margin %) × Projected Revenue

Maya's driver: 70% gross margin. So COGS = 30% × Summer 2 revenue.

03 / 10 · COGS
Category · Margins Driver 3 of 8

SG&A

Projection formula
Projected SG&A = SG&A % of Revenue × Projected Revenue

Maya's driver: 15% of revenue. SG&A scales with the top line.

04 / 10 · SG&A
Category · Capital & Tax Driver 4 of 8

Taxes

Projection formula
Projected Taxes = Tax Rate × Pre-Tax Income

Maya's driver: 21% tax rate. Applied to whatever pre-tax income the model produces.

05 / 10 · TAXES
Category · Investment Driver 5 of 8

CapEx

Projection formula
Projected CapEx = CapEx % of Revenue × Projected Revenue

Maya's driver: 4% of revenue. The bigger she gets, the more she reinvests in carts and equipment.

06 / 10 · CAPEX
Category · Investment Driver 6 of 8

D&A

Projection formula
Projected D&A = D&A % of Revenue × Projected Revenue

Maya's driver: 2% of revenue. Approximates the depreciation running off the asset base.

07 / 10 · D&A
Category · Working Capital Driver 7 of 8

Change in Working Capital

Projection formula
Projected ΔWC = WC % × (Projected Revenue − Prior Year Revenue)

Maya's driver: 10% of the change in revenue. Note the delta — working capital builds with the increase in sales, not the level.

08 / 10 · ΔWC
Category · Capital & Tax Driver 8 of 8

Interest

Projection formula
Projected Interest = Interest Rate × Debt Balance

Maya's driver: 6% interest rate. Applied to whatever debt sits on the balance sheet that year.
