Where the revenue lives — the long tail (text version)

This interactive uses Breaking Games' real Shopify sales data: 99 games, $61,085.33 in total revenue.

See the curve

Rank all 99 games by revenue, best to worst, and plot the running share of total revenue. The curve climbs steeply on the left, then flattens into a long flat tail on the right. The top 3 games already make 50.5% of all revenue; the top 10 make 74.5%; the top 25 make 89.3%. After about rank 13 the curve has nearly flattened — that bend is the cliff.

The long-tail question

The long tail is the flat stretch on the right. Correct reading (A): a few games make almost all the money, and most games barely sell. Not B (sales are not spread evenly) and not C (the bottom games are not about to catch up — together they make almost nothing).

Draw your cuts

Slide a cut line across the ranked games. Top N games make X% of revenue; the other 99 minus N make the rest. Three buckets shade the x-axis: Reorder heavy (the steep front), Maybe (the bend), Skip / let it ride (the flat tail). Where you draw that line is the spine of your reprint call.
