Module 3: What You May and May Not Do

Sort Six Actions Before Anyone Tells You the Rule

Here are six things you might do while assessing a client. Put each one into one of two columns, fine to do or needs written permission first.

Decide all six before you read on.

    6 left to place

    Passive is reading what is already published. Active is making a client’s systems or a client’s people do something for you.

    Passive needs nobody’s permission. Active always needs it, in writing, before you touch anything.

    Opening their public web page in your browser stays on the passive side. You are asking one machine for one page, the way its owner published it to be asked.
