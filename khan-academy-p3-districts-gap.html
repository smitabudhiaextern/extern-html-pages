Two counts of U.S. school districts, on one scale from zero to 12,500.

  • Districts that have Khan Academy's paid arrangement: 795, school year 2024 to 2025, from Khan Academy's annual report.
  • Districts that do not: about 12,500. This figure is worked out by subtraction, 13,318 minus 795, rounded. The national total of 13,318 regular public school districts is for 2022 to 2023, from the NCES Digest of Education Statistics, Table 214.10.

The two counts are two school years apart, so no percentage or ratio is given.
