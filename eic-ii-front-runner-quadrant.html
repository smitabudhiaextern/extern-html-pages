The rule

One proven core, one earlier white-space bet.

That pairing is the lesson. One front-runner is the proven, compounding core, already scaling and low risk. The other is the higher-upside, earlier bet in clearer white space: more risk, but potentially category-defining. Carrying both lets the market map size a proven category and a white-space opportunity. The most proven solution is often low-upside; the flashiest is often not real yet. Carry the two in between.
