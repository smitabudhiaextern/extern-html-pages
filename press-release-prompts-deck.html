Prompt 1 of 3
Set the persona.

Before you ask Claude anything about a filing, tell it who it is. The persona sets the voice and depth of every response that follows.

Prompt
You are an equity research analyst with 12 years of experience. You specialize in analyzing publicly traded companies across various industries. Your insights help institutional investors understand company fundamentals and make informed investment decisions.
Why this works
  • "Equity research analyst with 12 years of experience" — sets the role and seniority. Claude will respond like a senior analyst, not a generalist.
  • "Specialize in analyzing publicly traded companies" — narrows the lens to public equities (financial filings, earnings releases, SEC docs).
  • "Help institutional investors make informed decisions" — sets the audience. Output will be sharper, less hedged, written for someone who already knows the basics.
Prompt 2 of 3
Share context, then give the task.

With the persona set, point Claude at the artifact and tell it exactly what you want. Fill in the bracketed fields below — the prompt updates live, and Copy grabs your version.

Prompt

I've attached their and Full-Year earnings release.

Review the report and summarize the company's performance. Highlight what's strong, what's weak, and how this could affect the stock price in the short and long term. Please include direct quotes or excerpts from the earnings release to support your analysis.

Prompt 2 — Breakdown
Why each piece earns its place.
  • Company context — one or two sentences orienting Claude to what the company does. Without this, Claude has to infer the business model from the filing.
  • The artifact — explicitly tells Claude what's attached and when it covers. Anchors the response to the right time period.
  • The ask — three specific outputs: summarize performance, flag strengths and weaknesses, project stock impact. Each one is independently useful.
  • "Include direct quotes" — forces Claude to pull from the source, not paraphrase. Lets you tie out every claim to the filing.
Prompt 3 of 3
Simplify it.

Claude's first response will be dense — written for the analyst persona you set up. If you need it broken down further, this follow-up does it without losing the underlying analysis.

Prompt
Also, explain it to me like I'm new to finance, in simple language.
Why this works
  • "Like I'm new to finance" — tells Claude to translate technical terms (operating leverage, free cash flow, recurring revenue) into plain language.
  • "In simple language" — reinforces the register shift. Without it, Claude often just shortens the response without truly simplifying it.
  • No new task — you're not asking Claude to re-analyze. You're asking it to re-explain the same analysis at a different reading level.
