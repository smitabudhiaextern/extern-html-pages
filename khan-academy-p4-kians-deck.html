8 of 8 · What the three questions found

Three of the six, and the questions that found them

Three of the six were sound, and that is the part worth keeping. Kian's deck looks finished because most of it is. Polish is not evidence, and none of the three things that sink this deck is about design. All three would have taken him ten minutes.

Three questions to run on any deck

  1. What does this term stand for, and is it written out on the slide where it first appears?
  2. Where did this number come from, and why do you believe it?
  3. What is the one idea on this slide, and does the slide say which way you would go?

They take about a minute each. You run them on somebody else's deck the way you just did, and then on your own, which is harder and matters more.
