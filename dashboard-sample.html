Part V What we found
Five key findings

The five things the team needs to know first.

Each finding maps to a query in analysis/queries.sql. Numbers are reproducible from breaking_games.db.

01 · Concentration
Two-thirds of revenue came from a single franchise — Dwellings of Eldervale at $39,814 (65.2%). The Q4 reorder is a concentrated bet on Dwellings, not a balanced portfolio call.
02 · Paid social ROI
Meta paid social cost $329 per attributed conversion. Organic search delivered 3.9× the CVR at $0 of paid spend. The reallocation opportunity is hiding in plain sight.
03 · Cost-per-result spread
Within Meta, cost-per-result varies more than 14× between campaigns — from $4.96 on “Sales | ATC” to $70.76 on Advantage Plus. Worth an attribution audit before the next round of paid social budget locks for Q4.
04 · Retention gap
Only 12 of 1,037 unique customers came back for a second cart (1.2% repeat rate). The biggest untapped lever in the business isn't cart abandonment; it's building a retention engine that doesn't exist yet.
05 · POOP paradox
POOP variants pulled 17,000+ pageviews and produced 22 units. A page-conversion problem, not a print-quantity problem. The page itself is what to fix.
