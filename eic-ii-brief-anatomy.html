Getting More From Existing Wells: The Domestic-Energy Bet of the Decade
1
So-what: Every well starts declining the day it's drilled, and the industry spends about $540B a year just to hold production flat, so most oil is left in the ground.
2
The highest-leverage move is technology that recovers more from wells already in the ground.
3
Why now: Core oil-and-gas innovation is underfunded, so the cheapest barrel is one recovered from a well you already have. (GCV, 2025)
4
Who pays: Operators already spend heavily fighting decline, so a recovery gain pays for itself in barrels.
5
Key numbers: ~$540B/yr to hold production flat · ~30% more output per treated well.
6
Sources: (IEA/WSJ, 2025) · (Moleaer). Every number says where it came from.
