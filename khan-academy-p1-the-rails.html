1 A competing assessment Rail

Khan Academy is the official practice partner for the College Board's SAT Suite, a relationship that launched in 2015 and was announced at the time using the word "exclusively." The team does not build a matching free-practice partnership with a rival college-admissions test. If anything this one has got firmer, not softer: as of April 2026, districts running the full SAT Suite can get Khan Academy Districts for $10 per student.

Khan Academy blog · College Board · Khan Academy blog, April 2026

2 District sales Rail

Selling Khan Academy Districts into a specific, named school is the sales team's job. If a "partnership" idea is really just "convince this district to buy," it is not a partnership, and it is not your team's call to make.

3 Higher education Rail

Khan Academy tried to move into higher ed and ran into and other technical problems that made it impractical. The team is staying in K-12. There is no public writeup of this one. It is relayed the same way as the entries below, from what Sarah has told the team.

4 The learning-management integration

Khan Academy once built its Khanmigo teacher tools directly inside a major that most of its district base already used. Adoption of the platform itself was high. Adoption of the tools inside it was not, and the integration was . Khan Academy has never named the platform or published usage numbers, and neither does this document.

5 The assessment-data partnership

An used to sell Khan Academy's assessment-linked learning paths on Khan Academy's behalf. That relationship did not disappear so much as change hands. The underlying data connection is still live, and the resulting product, Learning Paths Informed by MAP Growth, is now a paid feature that Khan Academy's own sales team sells inside a Districts partnership.

6 Praxis

Khan Academy and ETS built Official Praxis Core Prep together starting in 2018, and it eventually served more than 2.3 million aspiring teachers. On February 27, 2024, ETS signed a new Praxis-prep partnership with Study.com instead, and Khan Academy removed Official Praxis Core Prep on April 30, 2024.

PR Newswire · Khan Academy Help Center
