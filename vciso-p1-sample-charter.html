Engagement charter

Client: Harlan Ridge Credit Union · Assessed service: member onboarding and account servicing · Consultant: vCISO Services, LLC

1. What this engagement covers

The process by which a person becomes a member and then manages their account: identity verification at branch and online, account creation in the core banking platform, and the member-facing web and mobile channels. It includes the six branch locations, the online banking front end, and the third parties involved in identity verification and card issuance.

2. What this engagement does not cover

Lending and underwriting, the ATM estate, payment processing and settlement, and corporate IT. These are scheduled separately. Where a system in one of those areas touches the assessed service, it has been recorded and flagged rather than assessed.

3. What you will deliver

  • An executive summary for the board
  • A report in six sections
  • Ten slides
  • The vendor's security questionnaire, all 27 questions answered, as an appendix

4. By when

Engagement conducted February to April, ahead of the annual examination and the core banking vendor's request for evidence.

Read this for size, not for content. None of Harlan Ridge's systems belong in your charter. Yours will be shorter, because your service is smaller than theirs. Sections 1 and 2 get written for you by the sorting screen. Sections 3 and 4 are yours.

Sections 1 and 2 are taken from Harlan Ridge's own report. Section 3 is what their package contains. Section 4 is the engagement period recorded on it.
