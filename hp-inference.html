What happens when AI answers you?

When you ask ChatGPT a question and it answers, that answering moment has a name: inference. Remember, training is how the AI learned, like studying for a test. Inference is the AI using what it learned to answer you, like taking the test.

The difference: studying happens once, but the AI takes the test every single time anyone asks it anything.

Why speed and cost matter so much

Here is the catch. Every answer costs a little money and takes a little time. One answer is no big deal. But a popular AI answers billions of questions, so those tiny costs and delays add up fast.

Startups race to make AI answer faster and cheaper. There are 2 main ways to do it. You will meet both, then pick which one you would back.
