Two filters, and Sarah says both of them out loud.

Asked what makes a partnership worth doing

It really just depends. We have a decent market share and high public trust, so we would want to ensure anyone we work with is similarly well-placed and trusted. That being said, if someone small has a really great opportunity, I wouldn't say no. It would just have to be financially beneficial for us. As a general rule, let's just say that we probably wouldn't do anything that didn't provide a minimum of $250K/year.

Sarah Thaler, Senior Strategic Partnerships Manager
