Filter two, the size bar
Wes's. Only Wes's.

Wes's rule of thumb. He does not take anything under about $250,000 a year to Sarah. Not greed, arithmetic: a deal eats the same nine to eighteen months of the same small team whether it is worth $60,000 or $600,000, so saying yes to the small one costs you the big one you could have chased instead.

This number is Wes's, and only Wes's. Khan Academy has never published a deal size it will or will not consider. It is one manager's read on what is worth his own time.
