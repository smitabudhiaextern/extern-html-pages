Three pitches, and the button that was never right

Two filters, in Sarah's order. Standing first: is this partner similarly well-placed and trusted? Then financial benefit: does what actually arrives make it worth doing?

One button was never the right answer. "Not financially beneficial enough" fits none of these three, and it is on screen because reading her $250K as a hard cut-off is the most common way to get pitch 1 wrong. Her words are "as a general rule", "let's just say" and "probably", and the sentence before the number says she would not say no to someone small with a really great opportunity. Treat it as the point where the conversation starts, not the point where it ends.

That is the end of this one. Nothing further is required.
