1
AI scouting
ChatGPT · Claude · Perplexity · Gemini — 15–30 min
▶
Prompt 1 — Set the persona
Tell the AI who to be before you ask it anything.
You are an experienced VC analyst at a venture fund. Your job is to scout startups matching a specific investment thesis. Be structured and data-driven. Use only recent, credible sources. If information is missing, say "Not available" — never guess. Acknowledge this role.
Prompt 2 — Feed it the thesis + your must-haves
Paste the HP Tech Ventures thesis, then lock the filters.
Here is the investment thesis: [paste thesis]. My non-negotiable filters: (1) US-based or does business in the US, (2) raised $1M–$40M total, (3) builds in the AI value chain, (4) my focus area: [Models / Inference / Applications], sub-area: [your pick]. Confirm you will only return startups matching ALL filters.
Prompt 3 — Scout 5, keep the best 3
List 5 startups that best match the thesis, with credible signals from the last 12 months. For each: name, website, one-line description, HQ, stage, why it passes each must-have, which nice-to-haves it hits, and 1–2 source links. Then tell me which 2–3 are the strongest fit and why. If information is unavailable, write "Not available" — do not invent anything.
⚠️ Trust, but verify
AI invents startups and funding rounds with total confidence. Before a company goes on your list, confirm it exists and check its funding on the company website, a news story, or Crunchbase. Ask the AI "show me your sources" — no sources, no shortlist. If one tool gives you nothing, switch tools before switching strategies.
2
The Core Search Stack
Where analysts find what AI misses — 20–40 min
▶
🎓 University & local accelerators — your unfair advantage
Early founders post publicly here, and these startups often aren't in AI training data yet. Nobody else in your cohort will find them.
"[your university] accelerator startups" · "[your university] demo day winners 2026"
🚀 YC & the big accelerators
Y Combinator's public Startup Directory is filterable by industry and keyword. Also: Techstars, 500 Global, Plug & Play, StartX, MassChallenge.
ycombinator.com → Companies → search "edge AI", "AI agents", "inference optimization"
💼 LinkedIn — startups before they're news
Founders announce funding and launches here first. Search your focus-area keywords, filter by Companies, then check size (1–50 employees) and recency.
LinkedIn search: "edge AI" → Companies filter → 1–50 employees · follow #buildinpublic
📺 YouTube — demo day is public
Founders demo prototypes at pitch events long before press coverage. Find a name → verify it with AI + Crunchbase.
"YC demo day AI pitch" · "edge AI startup demo" · "accelerator pitch day 2026 [your area]"

Product Hunt
Keyword-search your sub-area; sort by recent launches. Early products, founder comments, live traction signals.
Wellfound (ex-AngelList)
Discover tab → filter by AI + company size. Startups actively hiring are startups actively growing.
Reddit — r/startups, r/artificial, r/LocalLLaMA
Founders post launches and progress threads. LocalLLaMA is gold for edge/inference companies.
X (Twitter)
Follow @ycombinator, @paulg; search "#buildinpublic [your keyword]". Funding announcements land here first.
Plain Google, cleverly
"raised seed" OR "raised Series A" [your sub-area] 2026 -site:crunchbase.com
Still stuck after all this?
Loosen the nice-to-haves, never the must-haves. Try the adjacent sub-area inside your focus area. And say something in Slack — your PM and peers have solved this exact problem.
