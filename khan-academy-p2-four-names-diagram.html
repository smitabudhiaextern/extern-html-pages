The volunteers

  • Write and rewrite every article
  • Decide what counts as a trustworthy source
  • Decide what is worth an article at all
  • Settle their own arguments, in public

Do not: own the servers, the money or the name. Nobody pays them.

The Wikimedia Foundation

  • Runs and pays for the computers
  • Employs the lawyers
  • Owns and protects the Wikipedia name
  • Hands out grants to volunteers

Do not: write a single article, or decide what goes in one.

Wikimedia Enterprise The paid service. Not a fourteenth website, and not a different Wikipedia.

Notice what the boundary does. The Foundation sits inside Wikimedia, next to the volunteers, not above them. It pays for everything and writes none of it.
