How to Pick the Right Tool for Each Step

Here's the plan for the build.

5 decisions · 5 tools
2 / 6 STEP 01 Primary · Free

Build the model in Antigravity

This is the primary build tool — it's free, gives you Claude inside, and keeps all your project files and context in one place so you're not re-pasting the same information every time. If you also have paid Claude ($20/month), even better — you'll get higher usage limits. But the free tier is enough to finish the build.

3 / 6 STEP 02 Fallback · Free

Not comfortable with Antigravity? Use Claude web instead

You can build the whole model in browser-based Claude (claude.ai), but expect it to feel slightly harder — chats get long fast, and every new window you open, you have to paste the full context back in (company name, line items, 10-K data).

4 / 6 STEP 03 Audit · Paid

Have paid Claude? Use Claude for Excel for the audit pass — not for the build

Claude for Excel doesn't have internet access, so it can't pull data from a 10-K directly. But once your model is already in the sheet, it's excellent for auditing — it can read every cell, check your formulas, and flag issues line by line.

5 / 6 STEP 04 Side Questions · Free

Use ChatGPT for general questions and concept doubts

"What is gross margin again?", "explain working capital" — ask ChatGPT so you don't burn your free Claude limits on side conversations that aren't directly about the model.

6 / 6 STEP 05 Research · Free

Use Perplexity for pulling information from the web

Recent earnings numbers, news, market context, anything you can't find in the 10-K. Perplexity pulls cited sources from the live web, which is what makes it the right tool for research.
