The five types of risk

Worked through on McDonald's. Illustrative examples only, not real claims.

📉
Market

Customers, competitors, and demand shifting outside the company's walls.

e.g. A regional burger chain starts undercutting McDonald's on price in a key city.
⚙️
Execution

Whether the team can actually deliver the plan it has set.

e.g. A new kitchen-layout rollout runs months behind across franchise locations.
🖥️
Technical

Whether the product or technology works reliably at scale.

e.g. The self-order kiosks keep crashing during the lunch rush.
🧑‍💼
Team

Key people: gaps, dependencies, or departures that would hurt.

e.g. The exec who owns key supplier relationships leaves for a rival.
⚖️
Regulatory / Legal

Laws, rules, and compliance that could constrain the business.

e.g. A city bans a packaging material the chain relies on.
🔍
Areas to diligence further

Open topics your research could not confirm, framed as questions to answer before investing.

Example: How much of same-store sales growth came from price increases versus more customers?
🎤
Specific questions to ask the team

The direct questions you would put to the founders or leadership when you meet them.

Example: What's the payback period on a kiosk retrofit per location?
Illustrative examples, not real claims about McDonald's.
