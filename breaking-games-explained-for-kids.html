Project 1 · Week 1

The Pile of Messy Lists

Your very first day at the game shop 👋

Meet Jess 👋

It's your first day at a shop that sells board games 🎲 — the kind you play at a table with friends. Your work-buddy Jess shows you how the shop keeps track of everything: sales, customers, ads, products… all in six different lists she taped together a couple years ago. It worked for a while. Then it broke. 😅

The big mess 🗂️

Here's what you're walking into:

📒
6
Separate lists the shop's info lives in
⏱️
2 hrs
How long it takes Jess to answer ONE question
🤝
0
Lists that talk to each other nicely

What's in the six lists? 📋

Here's what each of the six lists actually holds:

🧾

Sales totals

How much money each game made, and how many sold.

📦

Orders

Every order that came in — who bought what.

🛒

Carts

Every shopping cart — including the ones people filled but never paid for.

🚪

Where visitors came from

Which "door" each visitor walked through — Facebook, Google, and more.

📣

Ad campaigns

How the Facebook & Instagram ads performed.

🎯

Ad groups

The smaller groups of ads inside each ad campaign.

The mystery 🕵️

Every time the boss asks something simple — "How much did we sell last month?" — Jess has to dig through all six lists by hand and add it up.

It takes hours, and it's super easy to make a mistake. There has to be a better way to keep all this stuff! 😵‍💫

Why this matters right now 🎄

More info pours in every single week, and the shop's busiest time of year — the holidays — is coming. If finding one answer already takes two hours, the shop is going to drown.

You have to fix this now, before it gets worse. 🦸

Your missions 🔍

You have 4 missions to tidy up the mess:

👋
Mission 1

Meet the team & tour the lists

See what info the shop keeps and where each piece lives across the six lists.

🗄️
Mission 2

Build the magic box

Set up a real database — think of it as one super-smart filing box — and pour all six lists into it.

💬
Mission 3

Learn the magic words

Ask the box questions using a simple language called SQL — and get answers back in seconds.

🏁
Mission 4

Win back the time

Answer in 30 seconds what used to take Jess two whole hours. That's a superpower!

Your turn: which way is smarter? 🎮

The boss needs last month's sales, fast. Tap the smarter way to find it. 👇

📚

Dig through 6 lists by hand

  • ⏱️ Takes: 2 hours
  • 😵 Mistakes: easy to make
🚫 Slow and risky! Adding up six lists by hand wastes the whole afternoon.
🗄️

Ask the database

  • ⏱️ Takes: 30 seconds
  • ✅ Answer: always exact
⭐ Smart! One little question, one instant, correct answer. This is the whole point of Project 1.

The prize 🏆

By the end of Week 1, you hand the shop:

✅

One tidy database holding all six lists in a single place.

💬

The power to ask it anything and get an answer in seconds.

⏱️

Hours saved every week — time Jess gets back for real work.

No more digging by hand. Mystery solved! 🎉

💡

One big idea to remember

Messy information is almost useless. The very first job of a data detective isn't fancy math — it's:

"Get everything into one clean, tidy place you can actually ask."

Tidy first, clever later. 🗄️

Project 2 · Week 2

The Name Game

One game, five different names 🤔

Meet the problem 👋

A customer clicks an ad, looks at a game called Dwellings of Eldervale, adds it to their cart, and buys it. Simple story, right? Except that one game is written a different way on four different lists — and not one of them uses the same name tag. So the computer thinks they're four different games! 🤯

The big mix-up 🔀

Here's the tangle you have to sort out:

🎲
117
Different games the shop sells
📋
4
Lists that should connect… but don't
🏷️
0
Shared name tags between them

The mystery 🕵️

Because every list spells the game a little differently, the computer can't follow one game's whole story from ad → cart → sale.

It's like four friends describing the same dog, but each using a different name — nobody realises it's the same dog! 🐶

Why this matters right now 🎄

Mike, the boss, needs to decide which of the 117 games to re-order for the holidays. If the lists won't connect, he can't tell which games actually sell.

Guess wrong and the shop runs out of the popular games — or wastes money on duds nobody wants. 😬

Your missions 🔍

You have 4 missions to make the lists hold hands:

🧩
Mission 1

Spot the problem

See exactly how one game gets split into four "different" games across the lists.

🏷️
Mission 2

Give every game an ID

Build ONE master list where each game gets a single official name tag (called a key).

🔗
Mission 3

Connect the lists

Teach the lists to hold hands using those tags (a JOIN) so you can follow one game start to finish.

🎁
Mission 4

Pick the winners

Add up the sales for each game and tell Mike exactly which ones to re-order.

Your turn: which setup connects? 🎮

You want the computer to follow one game's whole story. Tap the setup that lets it. 👇

🔤

Spell the name on each list

  • 📋 List 1: Dwellings of Eldervale
  • 📋 List 2: dwellings eldervale
  • 📋 List 3: DoE
🚫 Confusing! Three spellings = three "different" games to the computer. Nothing connects.
🏷️

Give it ONE tag everywhere

  • 📋 List 1: game #1
  • 📋 List 2: game #1
  • 📋 List 3: game #1
⭐ Smart! Same tag everywhere means the computer follows the game's whole story in one click.

The prize 🏆

By the end of Week 2, you hand Mike:

✅

One master list of all 117 games, each with its own ID.

🔗

All four lists connected — a game's full story is one click away.

🎁

A re-order plan Mike can actually act on for the holidays.

Now the shop buys the right games. Mystery solved! 🎉

💡

One big idea to remember

A computer can't tell two things are "the same" unless you give them a matching tag. The big trick of databases is simple:

"One thing = one ID — and suddenly everything connects."

Give it a name tag, and the whole story clicks together. 🔗

Project 3 · Weeks 3–4

The Great Game-Shop Money Mystery

Did the ads even work? 🕵️

Meet Mike 👋

Mike runs the shop's online store and wants lots of people to come and buy games. So he spent money to make that happen. 🎉

Mike's big idea (and big problem) 💡

To get people to visit, Mike paid for ads on the internet. Ads are like posters or commercials that pop up on Facebook and Instagram. Mike spent a LOT of money on them.

💸
$37,000
Money Mike spent on internet posters
🛒
$61,000
Money the shop made selling games
🚪
421
Different "doors" people came through

The mystery 🕵️

Here's the tricky part. When someone buys a game, Mike can't tell which poster made them come.

People don't walk in and say, "Hi! I'm here because I saw your Facebook poster!" They just show up. So Mike is stuck. Did the posters even work? He really isn't sure! 😵‍💫

Why this matters right now 🎄

The holidays are coming — Mike's busiest, most important time of the whole year. He's going to spend even more money on posters. But he doesn't want to waste it on posters nobody looks at.

He needs a plan. And that's where YOU come in. 🦸

Your missions 🔍

You have 4 missions to solve the mystery:

🔎
Mission 1

Find the real question

Before anything else, figure out exactly what Mike needs to know. Good detectives always ask the right question first!

🚪
Mission 2

Sort the visitors into piles

People came through 421 different doors — but really it's just a few BIG ones, like Facebook, Google, and Instagram. Put them into neat piles so they're easy to count.

🧮
Mission 3

Do the money math

For each poster, work out: was it cheap and worked great ⭐, or expensive and did nothing 🚫? Star the good ones. Cross out the bad ones.

🗣️
Mission 4

Tell Mike — nicely

Some posters didn't work. You have to tell Mike the truth, even when it's not happy news — but in a kind, clear way, using the numbers to back you up.

Your turn: which ad worked? 🎮

You're the detective now! Mike paid for these two posters. Tap the one you think was the smart buy. 👇

🐱

Sparkle Kitty Poster

  • 💸 Cost: $2,400
  • 👆 People who clicked: 3
🚫 Money waster! It cost a LOT and almost nobody clicked. Stop it!
🐉

Dragon Castle Poster

  • 💸 Cost: $300
  • 👆 People who clicked: 250
⭐ Smart buy! It was cheap AND tons of people clicked. Keep it!

The prize: Mike's plan 🏆

At the end, you hand Mike a plan. It tells him three things:

✅

Keep these posters — they worked! Don't change them.

❌

Stop these posters — they wasted money. Turn them off.

🎁

Spend the leftover money here — the smartest place to put it for the holidays.

Now Mike knows exactly what to do. Mystery solved! 🎉

💡

One big idea to remember

A real detective can't always be 100% sure. So instead of pretending you know everything, a smart detective says:

"Here's my best guess — and here's how sure I am."

Being honest about what you don't know makes people trust you more. That's the secret of a great detective! 🕵️‍♀️

Project 4 · Weeks 5–6

The Vanishing Shoppers

They filled their carts… then disappeared 💨

Meet the mystery 👋

Something strange is happening at the shop. Over 1,000 people filled up their online shopping carts this year… and then vanished without paying. Poof! 💨 That's a lot of money that almost happened. Why are they all leaving?

The vanishing act 🛒

Here's what the numbers look like — but watch out, some are sneaky:

🛒
1,000+
Carts filled up, then abandoned
🤖
?
How many were sneaky robots (you'll find out)
📉
?
The REAL leaving-rate, once bots are gone

The mystery 🕵️

At first it looks like tons of real customers are quitting. But look closely and some "shoppers" did weird things — like adding hundreds of items in a few seconds, or visiting 900 times in one minute.

Those aren't people. They're bots (robot programs), and they're making the numbers lie. 🤖

Why this matters right now 🎄

The holidays are almost here. If the shop believes the fake, bot-filled numbers, they'll fix the wrong things.

You need to clean out the robots and find the real story before the big rush — so the team spends its energy where it actually counts. 🦸

Your missions 🔍

You have 4 missions to find the truth:

💡
Mission 1

Guess why people leave

Make smart hypotheses (a fancy word for "good guesses") about why real shoppers quit before paying.

🔎
Mission 2

Hunt for patterns

Look for clues: which products get dropped, what times of day, what keeps repeating?

🤖
Mission 3

Spot the robots

Find the fake shoppers and build a clean filter that hides them from every future count.

📉
Mission 4

Find the real number

Re-count without the bots, and tell the team the honest leaving-rate — plus what they can fix.

Your turn: real shopper or sneaky bot? 🎮

One of these "shoppers" is actually a robot. Tap the one you'd ignore in your count. 👇

🧑

Shopper A

  • 👀 Looked at: 4 games
  • 🛒 Added to cart: 1 game
  • ⏱️ Took: 6 minutes
🧑 This is a normal human! Keep them in your count — they're a real customer.
🤖

Shopper B

  • ⚡ Visited: 900 times in 60 sec
  • 🛒 Filled: 300 carts
  • 📧 Email: none
⭐ Caught it! No real person shops like this. It's a bot — ignore it so the numbers stay honest.

The prize 🏆

By the end of the project, you hand the team:

✅

A clean filter that hides bots from every future question.

📉

The real cart-abandonment number — robots removed.

🛠️

A short list of fixes the shop can actually make before the holidays.

Honest numbers, real fixes. Mystery solved! 🎉

💡

One big idea to remember

Not every number is telling the truth. Before you trust the data, a good detective asks:

"Is this even a real person?"

Cleaning out the fakes is what makes the rest of your answer trustworthy. 🤖🚫

Project 5 · Weeks 7–8 · The Grand Finale

The Big Show

Now tell the whole team what you found 🌟

Meet the moment 👋

You've cracked every case. But here's the thing — all your brilliant answers are stuck in your head and your computer. In 10 days the whole team gathers for a big meeting, including Shari, the founder and big boss. They need to SEE what you found, all on one page. This is your moment. 🌟

The countdown ⏳

The clock is ticking:

🗓️
10
Days until the big team meeting
🛍️
12
Days until Black Friday (the busiest day)
📊
1
Single page that has to tell the whole story

The mystery 🕵️

A pile of answers isn't a story. If you just dump 50 numbers on a screen, everyone's eyes glaze over. 😴

The challenge: turn everything you discovered into one clear picture a busy founder can understand in 90 seconds — and be ready to defend it when they ask tough questions.

Why this matters right now 🎄

This is the finale. Black Friday is days away, real money is on the line, and the team has to agree on the Holiday Game Plan — your plan.

Nail this and the whole journey pays off, with your name on the work. 🏆

Your missions 🔍

You have 4 missions to put on the big show:

🎯
Mission 1

Pick what matters

Choose the few numbers that actually change a decision — and toss the show-off ones nobody acts on.

📊
Mission 2

Build the dashboard

Make one colorful page with charts so the whole story is obvious at a glance.

🎤
Mission 3

Practice the talk

Get ready to explain it in 10 minutes and answer tough questions calmly and clearly.

🏁
Mission 4

Deliver

Present your Holiday Game Plan to the whole team, start to finish. Done!

Your turn: which screen tells the story? 🎮

🌫️

Screen A

Screen B

  • 🗣️ Boss says: "Got it — let's do it."
⭐ Smart! A few clear numbers, a chart, and one recommendation. The story lands in seconds.

The prize 🏆

By the end of the finale, you walk away with:

✅

A one-page holiday dashboard with clear charts.

🎤

A 10-minute talk you can defend in the room.

🏆

Your finished Holiday Game Plan — a real portfolio piece with your name on it.

You didn't just find answers — you made the whole team act on them. That's the job! 🎉

💡

One big idea to remember

Finding the answer is only half the work. The other half is:

"Tell the story so clearly that people actually do something about it."

A detective who can't explain the case never closes it. 🎤
