What is an AI app?

You already learned that a model is the AI brain. An application is the actual product you open and use, built on top of that brain. ChatGPT is an app. The AI filter that changes your selfie is an app. The AI that helps you write is an app.

The brain does the thinking. The app is the thing you actually touch and use.
