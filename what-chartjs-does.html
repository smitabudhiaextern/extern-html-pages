What this means for your dashboard

Both of your charts are already built

Your template already has Chart.js switched on and both charts already set up. The chart types are chosen, the colours are chosen, the names are written. There is nothing to install and nothing to download.

The only thing wrong with them is the numbers, because they are examples rather than yours. Replacing those numbers is the entire job in this module.
