Why Could It Go Up or Down?

Every stock has reasons it might rise (bull case) and reasons it might fall (bear case). Tap the ones you find most convincing for AAPL.

"Invert, always invert. Turn a situation or problem upside down. Look at it backward."
— Charlie Munger, Vice Chairman, Berkshire Hathaway
🧠
Coach tip: "Bull" = optimistic (like a bull charging up). "Bear" = pessimistic (like a bear swiping down). Pick at least 2 from each side. Real analysts always consider both — that's what makes them credible.
