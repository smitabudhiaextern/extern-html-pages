The Four Services

Cumberland’s work splits into four parts. Each part is called a service, which is close to what most companies call a department: a job the company does, the team that does it, and the computers they do it on.

Dana cannot afford to check all four before the Meridian contract renews, so she is taking one this quarter and letting you pick which one. Whatever you pick is your job for the whole program. Assessing a service means working out what it runs on, who touches it, and what Cumberland can prove about keeping it safe.

Read all four. Each one comes with a picture of how the work moves and a list of the four systems you would spend eight weeks on. Some words will be new.
