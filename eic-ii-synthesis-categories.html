The six synergy categories

Same categories, worked through on McDonald's. Illustrative examples only, not a real fund's actual view.

Thesis Synergy

Does what they build, and the problem it solves, map to the fund's focus?

McDonald's: a fund that backs "convenient, affordable food access" would see McDonald's drive-thru speed and price point as a direct thesis match.
Market Synergy

Does the market they sit in, and how far their reach extends, match where the fund wants exposure?

McDonald's: scale in fast-growing international markets extends the fund's exposure to where quick-service dining is expanding fastest.
Technology Synergy

Is their technical edge, and how defensible it is, the kind of edge the fund backs?

McDonald's: kitchen automation and app-based ordering data give McDonald's a defensible operational edge competitors can't easily copy.
Business-Model Synergy

Does the way they make money and scale match how the fund likes companies to grow?

McDonald's: the franchise model scales with low incremental capital from McDonald's itself, which a fund favoring capital-efficient growth would like.
Team Synergy

Do the founders have real, verifiable credibility on exactly this problem?

McDonald's: leadership with deep operating experience in restaurant logistics is exactly the credibility a fund backing supply-chain efficiency would want to see.
Stage & Signal Synergy

Is the stage right, and are outside players validating it?

McDonald's: continued expansion and strong franchisee demand for new locations is the kind of external validation a growth-stage fund looks for.
