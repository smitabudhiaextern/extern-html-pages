Dimensions & Facts
Two kinds of tables

The menu describes. The orders record.

You've been working with two pizza sheets. Look closer and they do completely different jobs. One says what each pizza IS. The other logs what actually HAPPENED. That split is the backbone of every database you'll build.

The menu = a DIMENSION
Describes what each pizza is. One row per pizza. Small and stable — it barely changes.
The orders = a FACT
Records what happened. One row per order. The list only grows.
